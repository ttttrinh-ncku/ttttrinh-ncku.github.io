About Me

I am a researcher working on AI-driven microalgae systems for carbon capture, wastewater treatment, and bioenergy production. My work integrates biotechnology, machine learning, and environmental engineering.

Research

AI-Controlled Multi-Species Microalgae System

Developing a machine learning-driven framework to optimize species composition and environmental parameters for enhanced biomass productivity and CO₂ fixation.

  • Goal: Maximize carbon capture efficiency

PFAS Removal from Industrial Wastewater

Investigating microalgae-based remediation strategies for removing persistent pollutants (PFAS) in semiconductor wastewater.

  • Approach: Bioadsorption + biodegradation
  • Application: Industrial wastewater treatment

Artificial Algal Bloom System (Blue Hole Concept)

Designing a large-scale floating system that utilizes nutrient upwelling to induce controlled algal blooms for carbon sequestration.

  • Concept: Ocean Spiral-inspired system
  • Goal: Climate change mitigation
